# Reserved for Lacey

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Add the personal bits

- Put your song at `assets/song.mp3`.
- Put your favorite photo of Lacey at `assets/lacey.jpg`.
- Change any wording directly in `index.html` if you want it more personal.

The opening plays automatically after “I am Lacey,” then the five personal chapters support buttons, arrow keys, and phone swipes. The final page can replay the whole film.
